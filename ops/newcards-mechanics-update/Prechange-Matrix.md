## Evidence and acceptance gate

The rule engine exports only confirmed tags at confidence 0.90 or higher. `CardStrategyTagsRow` and the Supabase payload have no suggestion map; a remote hit resolves to an empty suggestion list. This specifically suppresses `graveyard_enabler` (0.80), `death_payoff` (0.88), and `counters_source` (0.80) unless another local path recomputes them.

Run the 22-fixture `DeckAnalysisV3CorpusTest` corpus and the realistic-density `DeckAnalysisEngineCalibrationTest` before accepting new 6-producer/4-payoff Commander ideals. The `WizardCommanderHarnessV2Test` exercises 180 real-collection specifications. Capture section id, attributed eligible card ids, Collection matches, global query, and expected global examples in the follow-up verification report. Do not label an empty category a defect without at least one eligible card its own predicate accepts.
